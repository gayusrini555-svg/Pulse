Questions, answered

What does Pulse actually do?

It studies the posts you have already published, finds the persuasion levers you lean on and the creator you are becoming, listens to what your audience is asking, and helps you run small experiments with fresh ideas. Over time it tells you which levers really work for your audience, based on your own results.

Why do I connect Buffer?

Pulse reads the posts you have sent through your Buffer account, and can save ideas and drafts back to it. You sign in with Buffer and approve what Pulse can see. A free Buffer account works.

Which platforms does it support?

LinkedIn for now, where thoughtful posts matter most. More platforms will follow.

Will Pulse post for me?

Never on its own. You choose whether an idea or draft is saved, and you edit and schedule it yourself.

Why do I bring my own results?

Performance numbers are not shared with outside apps by default. So you choose: upload your LinkedIn analytics file, or paste a Buffer key that Pulse uses once to read your numbers and then throws away.

Is my data safe?

Pulse reads only what it needs, never sells or shares your data, and lets you delete everything in one click. Keys are never stored.

Which AI does Pulse use?

A leading AI model (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude or OpenAI GPT) codes your posts, groups audience questions and drafts text. Your text is sent to it only for that, and every label links back to the post it came from.

Where do the archetypes come from?

From a codebook built on published research on persuasion and sharing. The archetype is a friendly summary of your lever mix, not a personality test.

Is this an official Buffer product?

No. Pulse is an independent app built on the Buffer API for the Buffer Commit 2026 contest.

How much does it cost?
