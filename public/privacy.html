Privacy notes

Last updated 12 November 2026. Written in plain words. Pulse is an independent project built on the Buffer API for Buffer Commit 2026 and is not an official Buffer product.

What Pulse reads

When you connect your Buffer account and approve access, Pulse reads your account name and organizations, the channels you connected, and the text and dates of posts you have sent on the channel you choose. Pulse can also save ideas and drafts to your Buffer when you click a button that says so.

What Pulse stores

Your onboarding answers, the research labels Pulse gives your posts (with a short excerpt of each post), the audience pain points and ideas it finds, your experiments and predictions, and the performance numbers you choose to share. These are stored in a secured database, linked to your Buffer account ID.

Your results and keys

You can share performance numbers by uploading an analytics file or by pasting a Buffer personal key. A key is used once, for that request only, to read your post numbers. It is never saved or logged.

AI processing

To code posts, find pain points and write drafts, Pulse sends the relevant text to an AI provider (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude or OpenAI, depending on configuration). It is sent only to do that job.

What Pulse never does

Pulse never sells or shares your data, never posts without your click, and never reads private messages.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete my data" in the account menu inside the app. It removes everything Pulse stored about you and signs you out. You can also revoke Pulse's access from your Buffer settings at any time.
